== CLASSIFICATION RULES ==
- collector: REAL personal owner of watches. Look for wrist shots, casual lifestyle photos with watches naturally in frame, watches paired with the person's other belongings (car, watch winder, home). NOT product shots.
- dealer: business account selling watches — inventory shots on stands/displays, pricing language, "DM for price", "for sale", store photos
- hnw_no_watches: zero watches visible, but clear ultra-luxury wealth signals (jets, yachts, hypercars, Birkins, CEOs/founders of major brands)
- replica_dealer: bio/captions mention replica keywords (replica, 1:1, superclone, AAA, dhgate, fake, mirror quality, rep watch, reptime, factory direct, "clean factory", "noob factory", "VS factory")
- irrelevant: no watches AND no UHNI signals, OR service businesses (repair, insurance, auth), OR aftermarket modification businesses (gem-setting, custom dials, strap makers, watch winders, watch boxes, photographers)

== "REAL OWNERSHIP" VS "JUST IMAGES" — CRITICAL ==
Many profiles post watch IMAGES they don't own. These are NOT collectors:
- Pinterest-style aesthetic accounts that aggregate watch photos
- News/blog accounts (Hodinkee-style content)
- Generic luxury lifestyle accounts that screenshot watches from sales sites
- Stock-photo / promotional images with clean white backgrounds

Signs of REAL ownership:
- Wrist shot showing the watch on someone's actual wrist
- Watch visible in genuine lifestyle context (in a car, at dinner, on a desk with personal items)
- Selfie or mirror photo showing the person wearing it
- Multiple shots of same watch in different real settings
- Watch winder / display case at home with their items

Signs of NON-ownership (mark as irrelevant or low rank):
- Clean product/stock images (white background, professional photography)
- Watermarks from other accounts
- Screenshot of e-commerce or social media post
- Caption mentions another brand/account as image source

If unsure, default to LOWER rank and lower confidence.

== VINTAGE WATCH GUARDRAIL ==
Vintage simple watches (e.g., vintage Rolex Oyster, vintage Omega, vintage Cartier Tank, vintage Patek Calatrava without complications) are NOT automatically rank 8-9 just because they're vintage.
- Vintage Patek Calatrava (3-hand, no complications) = score by brand tier only (Tier S = 8 base, but only if confirmed owned)
- Vintage Rolex Oyster/Datejust = standard Tier B (4-5), NOT elevated
- Vintage value depends on rarity (e.g., Paul Newman Daytona = 8), not age alone
- Generic vintage 3-hand watches from collectors who have a few of them = 4-6, NOT 8-9
