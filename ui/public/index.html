1
Seed Accounts (Google Sheets)
Curated luxury watch accounts as starting points
Source: "Seeds" tab (Sheet1) in Google Sheets
Accounts: ~120+ curated luxury watch Instagram accounts (grows via Seed Loop)
Target Brands: Richard Mille, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, Rolex, F.P. Journe, MB&F, A. Lange & Sohne, Vacheron Constantin
Purpose: These are known watch accounts whose followers are likely collectors/enthusiasts
2
Scrape Followers (Apify)
Up to 50 followers per seed account
Tool: Apify Instagram Follower Scraper
Limit: 50 followers per seed (balance between coverage & cost)
Output: List of Instagram usernames following each seed
Deduplication: Cross-seed dedup prevents the same follower being scraped twice
3
Pre-Filter (no AI cost)
Drop private accounts, replicas, low-activity
Filters Applied:
- Remove private accounts
- Replica keyword pre-filter (replica, 1:1, superclone, fake, dhgate, etc.)
- Min 50 followers (basic activity threshold)
- Cross-seed deduplication (skip already-scraped usernames)
4
Apify Profile Scrape (10 posts)
For each follower: bio + 10 most recent posts
Enrichment per profile:
- Bio, follower count, post count, verified/business flags
- Email + phone extracted from bio
- 10 most recent posts (display image + caption per post)
- Video URLs separated and excluded from AI (Gemini hallucinated on videos)

Output: in-memory profile object passed directly into Stage 1 Triage (no intermediate storage)
5
Stage 1 — Fast Triage (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
Lightweight ~900-token prompt, 10 posts, just classify + rank
Goal: Cheap, fast classification — route profiles to the right sheet before spending on deep analysis.

Model: Gemini 2.5 Flash
Input: 10 post images + bio + recent captions
Prompt size: ~900 tokens (64% smaller than the old heavy prompt)
Output fields: classification, visible_watch_rank (0-10), net_worth_tier, priority_score, confidence, reasoning

Hard rules:
- Replica keywords → priority=0, classification="replica_dealer"
- 0 watches + UHNI signals → classification="hnw_no_watches"
- 0 watches + no UHNI signals → classification="irrelevant"

Priority formula: MAX(visible_watch_rank, wealth_tier_to_num(net_worth_tier)); +1 if both ≥ 7
6
n8n Router (Switch Node)
Routes Stage 1 output to one of three sheets
Based on classification + visible_watch_rank + net_worth_tier:
- Sheet A if (collector OR dealer) AND visible_watch_rank ≥ 6
- Sheet B if hnw_no_watches AND net_worth_tier = ultra-high
- Sheet C otherwise (irrelevant, replica, low rank)
Sheet A
A
Collectors / Dealers
Stage 1 triage list — visible_watch_rank 6-10
Sheet A (20 cols, Stage 1 only): username, full_name, classification, visible_watch_rank, net_worth_tier, priority_score, confidence, profile_url, follower_count, post_count, is_verified, is_business, business_category, bio, email_in_bio, phone_in_bio, external_url, seed_account, seed_type, scrape_date.

Triggers deep dives on visible_watch_rank ≥ 8 → results land in Sheet A - Deep Dive (37 cols: Stage 1 + Horology + Wealth + Lifestyle).
Sheet B
B
Watchless UHNI
UHNWI network — no watches but ultra-high wealth
Sheet B (20 cols, Stage 1 only): same triage fields as Sheet A.

No horology data (no watches to analyze).
Triggers Wealth + Lifestyle deep dives on every entry → results land in Sheet B - Deep Dive (32 cols).

UHNWI gate: if Wealth deep dive returns non-Ultra-High → row auto-moves to Sheet C.
Sheet C
C
Low Rank
Rank 1-5, irrelevant, replica — record-keeping only
Minimal columns: username, full_name, classification, visible_watch_rank, net_worth_tier, priority_score, confidence, profile_url, follower_count, scrape_date, seed_account.
No deep dive triggered. Workflow ends here.
7
Stage 2 — Deep Dive (Rank 8-10 / UHNI only)
Two n8n workflows: Horology + Wealth/Lifestyle (3 prompt kinds total)
Trigger: Sheet A profiles with visible_watch_rank ≥ 8, OR all of Sheet B (UHNI).
Image fetch: Fresh Apify scrape — up to 50 images per profile (vs 10 in Stage 1).

Workflow 2a — Horology (Sheet A 8-10 only, Gemini 2.5 Pro):
watch_brands, specific_watches (brand + model + reference + value), watch_complications, watch_complication_tier (Tier 1/2/3), visible_recent_collection_value → written to Sheet A - Deep Dive.

Workflow 2b — Wealth + Lifestyle (Sheet A 8-10 + Sheet B, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Wealth runs before Lifestyle per user):
- Wealth: visible_wealth_tier, profession, business_sector (Real Estate / Crypto / Finance / Tech / Family Office / etc.), wealth_signals, approach_suggestion
- Lifestyle: inferred_lifestyle_value, hobbies (Motorsport / Polo / Sailing / etc.), locations (Monaco / Aspen / Dubai), dining_preferences, vision_summary
Results → Sheet A - Deep Dive (for Sheet A profiles) or Sheet B - Deep Dive (for Sheet B profiles).

UHNWI gate (Lifestyle): Lifestyle runs only if Wealth confirms Ultra-High in BOTH visible_wealth_tier AND net_worth_tier.
Cost: ~5x Stage 1 per profile (50 images vs 10, Pro for Horology, fresh Apify scrape) — but only ~15-20% of profiles trigger it.
8
Seed Loop — Flywheel
Verified Rank 8-10 / UHNI → scrape THEIR following → new seeds
Trigger: Sheet A profiles with verified visible_watch_rank ≥ 8, OR Sheet B UHNI entries.
Action: Auto-push the username back into the Seeds sheet with tag seed_type = follower_loop.
Then: Apify scrapes the accounts that THIS target follows (their following list) — these are highly likely to be other collectors/UHNI.
Result: Self-growing collector base — Sheet A and Sheet B compound over time without manual seed curation.
