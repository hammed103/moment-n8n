===== WATCH COMPLICATION IDENTIFICATION =====
Analyze the dial of any visible watches and identify the complications present. Then assign the HIGHEST complication tier detected across all watches.

TIER 1 - HIGH-COMPLICATION (watch_complication_tier = "Tier 1"):
Pinnacle of watchmaking — hundreds of components, immense assembly time.
- Minute Repeater: chimes the time with tiny hammers and gongs (visible gong/slide on case side)
- Tourbillon: rotating cage around escapement (visible open aperture on dial showing spinning cage)
- Perpetual Calendar: day + date + month + moonphase (multiple sub-dials + leap year indicator)
- Split-Seconds Chronograph / Rattrapante: two superimposed chrono hands
- Grande / Petite Sonnerie: automatic chiming (gong/slide visible)
- Celestial / Sky Chart: rotating star map on dial

TIER 2 - MID-COMPLICATION (watch_complication_tier = "Tier 2"):
Highly respected, technically impressive, practical for daily/travel wear.
- Annual Calendar: day + date + month (usually 3 apertures or sub-dials)
- Retrograde: arc-shaped hand that snaps back
- Standard Chronograph: stopwatch (e.g., Rolex Daytona, Omega Speedmaster — sub-dials for minutes/seconds)
- Flyback Chronograph: like standard chrono but with reset-on-fly
- World Time: 24 city names on dial (e.g., Patek 5230)
- GMT / Dual Time: second hour hand, usually 24-hour (e.g., Rolex GMT-Master II)
- Moonphase: lunar cycle disc visible on dial

TIER 3 - BASIC (watch_complication_tier = "Tier 3"):
Foundational elements — legibility, reliability, everyday utility.
- Three-Hand / Center Seconds: hours, minutes, sweeping seconds
- Small Seconds: hours + minutes center, seconds in sub-dial at 6
- Two-Hand: hours + minutes only (e.g., classic Cartier Tank, Calatrava)
- Date Window: simple date aperture
- Day-Date: day + date (e.g., Rolex Day-Date)
- Power Reserve Indicator: gauge showing spring tension
